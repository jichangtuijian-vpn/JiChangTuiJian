## 📖 Buying Guide

### Before You Buy

| Tip | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| 🆕 **For Beginners** | Start with a monthly or short-term plan before committing to longer periods |
| 📡 **Line Selection** | Relay/Premium lines usually outperform direct connections, but experience varies by region and ISP |
| 🔄 **Backup Service** | Always have a backup service — don't put all your needs on one provider |
| 🎯 **Special Needs** | AI/Emby/Xinjiang/Unlimited devices/Unlimited traffic — check the Specialty Series |
| 🌱 **Growing Services** | Experienced users may consider newer services — cheaper and faster, but higher risk |

### FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Direct, Relay, or Premium Line?</b></summary>

Relay services are recommended for better bandwidth. Premium lines have the advantage of not passing through the firewall, resulting in lower latency.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What matters most when evaluating a service?</b></summary>

**Bandwidth and stability are the most important factors**, followed by node count, line quality, and customer support responsiveness.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does the protocol matter?</b></summary>

For relay services, the protocol is less important than the stability and bandwidth of the relay servers.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I buy based on rankings?</b></summary>

Experience varies by region, ISP, and time. We recommend trying a short-term plan first.
</details>
